About me

UX design student with a love for animals, art and accessible design.

Hi, I'm Emma

A bit about me

I'm a UX design student at TUC Yrkeshögskola since fall 2025, with a strong interest in usability and inclusivity. My studies have given me an analytical mindset and a solid understanding of how systems are designed and built – and I like to work inclusively, iteratively and with empathy for the people I design for.

Outside of UX I have a big passion for animals, video games and movies – my horse and my cat are the loves of my life. I also draw and paint, both digitally and traditionally, mostly portraits of people and horses.
