Underbara Unge – Redesign

Redesign (school project)Redesign|School project|UX/UI design|Figma

A school redesign of a memory-book app for parents, combining user-centred research with strict requirements for accessibility and sustainability.

01

The project

This is a school project where the goal was to redesign and improve an already existing service, by combining user-centred research with strict requirements for accessibility and sustainability.

The mobile app Underbara Unge is made for parents to create a personal memory book of their child's first year.

02

The solution

The final design lowers the threshold for the user with flexible steps and motivational progression. These improvements create a more inclusive and undemanding experience that better supports the user's everyday life and increases the likelihood that the book will be completed.

WCAG AA

The new design is more accessible thanks to the change of colour scheme – the new colours reach the WCAG AA standards.
