Equestrian Management App

App concept|UX/UI design|Figma|Solo

In progress

An app that collects conversations, bookings and planning for horse owners, stable owners and staff in one place.

01

The idea

An idea that arose when I noticed myself keeping up with multiple conversations, bookings and planning across different platforms. That's why I wanted to create an app that collected everything in one place.

Horse owners, stable owners and employees can access and review everything from veterinary appointments to contacting other horse owners in the same stable.

02

Next step

The app is still under construction and currently just an idea. The next step in the work process is to conduct field studies, interviews and investigate what the need actually looks like!
